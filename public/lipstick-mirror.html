mirror,
mirror.

Pinch your thumb and finger in the air to draw lipstick on your reflection. Your camera stays on your device.

How to

Draw

Pinch thumb + index finger of your right hand. Move to draw, release to stop. Mouse works too.

Erase

Pinch with your other hand while you draw — the dashed ring wipes the lipstick away. With a mouse, hold Shift. undo / clear also work.

Change colour

Hover over a shade until the ring fills. Or click it.

Already on the glass

A heart and a few xo are written for you every time. Erase them, draw over them, or press clear.

Snap to clear

Snap your fingers (thumb + middle finger, quick flick) and everything dissolves into sparkles. Clear does the same. undo brings it back.

Hands the wrong way round?

Press S or to flip which hand draws.

Fix your hair

Turn off hand tracking so the mirror ignores your hands. Turn it back on to draw.

Take photos

Press the big button (or make an L with both hands and hold). It counts 3‑2‑1, snaps, waits a moment, counts again, snaps — then prints your strip. Add kisses, then save.

Keyboard controls

S — swap hands · C — clear · Space / Enter — photo · 1–6 — colour · [ ] / ↑ ↓ — switch colours · N / L / K / V / B — filters · H — hand tracking · P — palette · ⌘/Ctrl + Z — undo · Esc — close
